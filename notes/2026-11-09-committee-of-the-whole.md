# SASD Board Meeting Notes — November 9, 2026

## Committee of the Whole

### Public input

- Community speakers advocated for agricultural education and FFA, emphasizing student opportunities, career preparation, community ties, and the importance of appropriate program facilities. [Audio approximately 00:00–00:30]

### School Perceptions community survey

- School Perceptions reported **1,467 responses**, an **18.3%** response rate, and a stated **±2.62% margin of error**. [Presentation; audio approximately 00:30–00:56]
- The presentation described weighted support for an operational referendum amount below the tested **$1.5 million** level as roughly 50%, while cautioning that the result is within the margin of error.
- Shared priorities across respondent groups included recruiting and retaining staff, student achievement, and student behavior/discipline.
- The Board discussed whether a possible April 2027 operational referendum should proceed or whether the District should wait for potential state school-finance changes.
- **No referendum decision was made.** Administration will return with discussion of referendum next steps, survey priority areas, and additional stakeholder input. [Audio approximately 01:04–01:29]

### Financial and facility reports

- The October financial update described limited cash receipts before December, three payrolls during October, and no unexpected expenditures.
- The reported fund balance was approximately **10% of expenditures** at the end of October, compared with a stated **18% policy level**. Administration expected to reach December without borrowing, though invoice timing required attention. [Audio approximately 00:56–01:00]
- Facility work at Meadowview and the high school was reported as progressing, with remaining furniture, door/window, weather-protection, and construction-schedule items under management. [Audio approximately 01:00–01:04]

### Library Media Center Plan

- The Board approved the four-year Library Media Center Plan. The plan addresses collection development, digital citizenship/media literacy/AI, makerspaces, and a K–12 robotics progression. [Audio approximately 01:29–01:40]

### e-Rate projects

- The Board approved the e-Rate project plan, which prioritizes a firewall replacement and allows limited potential network-equipment needs connected to high-school space changes.
- The discussion clarified that this approval supports the e-Rate application process and is not an immediate new appropriation. [Audio approximately 01:40–01:46]

### July 2027 holiday observance

- The Board approved moving observance of the July 4, 2027 holiday to **Friday, July 2, 2027**.
- The stated purpose was to allow traditional summer school to run July 5–30 without losing the Monday to the federal holiday observance. [Audio approximately 01:46–01:50]
